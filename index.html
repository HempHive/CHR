Casino

Roulette, Texas Hold’em, blackjack, and the fruit machine use the same player banks. The race stays paused while a game is open. Straight pays 35:1, splits 17:1, streets 11:1, corners 8:1, six-lines 5:1, dozens and columns 2:1, and the even-money bets pay 1:1.
