    Arcade Racing

    Settings

    The grandstand board uses this club’s league. Click that board for the table, then again for the next match. A third click returns to the race.

    Betting

    Tap a horse to add 10 on a win. Tap Place under that horse to add 10 on a place. Tap again to add another 10. Clear Bets removes that player’s wagers. +50 and +100 add to the bank.

    A win pays stake × the horse’s odds. A place pays if the horse finishes in the places. With eight runners that is the first three, at one fifth of the win odds. In a smaller field it is the first two, at one quarter of the win odds.

    Trials

    The top two from Trial A and the top two from Trial B go through to the Final.

    The race

    View switches between the flat track and the 3D broadcast. Laps sets 1, 2, or 3 circuits. The camera button on the picture cycles the broadcast view.

    Casino

    Roulette, Texas Hold’em, blackjack, baccarat, the fruit machine, Connect 4, Checkers, Backgammon, and Mahjong use the same player banks. Arrow buttons step through games in the same order as the top buttons. Darts is the target button: click a spot on the board, then tap that same spot again to throw. A quick second tap hits. A slow one falls short. Table Skittles is the cheese button after Darts: Aim mode works like Darts, or switch to Flick and drag the cheese. Score is solo. Match is first to 31 against the computer on Easy, Match, or Pro. Backgammon is the circle button after Skittles: you play gold, hits go to the bar, and Easy, Match, or Pro sets the house. Pub Quiz is the beer button after Backgammon: pay 50 to climb a 15-question ladder to 5000, with safe havens at 50 and 500. The big money sits in the last five. Phone a friend adds time, 50/50 removes two wrong answers, and replace swaps the question once. Walk away keeps what you have won. The race stays paused while a game is open. Straight pays 35:1, splits 17:1, streets 11:1, corners 8:1, six-lines 5:1, dozens and columns 2:1, and the even-money bets pay 1:1. Baccarat pays 1:1 on Player, 19:20 on Banker, and 8:1 on Tie. Connect 4, Checkers, Backgammon, and Mahjong pay even money. Checkers is the black piece button: you play black, jumps are forced, and Easy, Match, or Pro sets the house strength.
